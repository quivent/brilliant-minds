### Signature Contributions

1. **LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)** - A parameter-efficient fine-tuning method that enables adapting large language models without updating all parameters. Now "one of the most popular methods for customizing AI models." Developed to solve the problem that fine-tuning LLMs was becoming increasingly untenable - slow, expensive, and requiring enormous computational resources.

2. **muTransfer (muP/Maximal Update Parametrization)** - A technique for transferring optimal hyperparameters from small models to large models, solving the prohibitive cost of hyperparameter tuning at scale. Now "underpinning the largest AI models being developed today."

3. **GFlowNets Research** - Collaborative work on Generative Flow Networks as tools for developing reasoning and planning AI systems, exploring how models can autonomously discover concepts and their relationships.
