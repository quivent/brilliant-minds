## Core Contribution to Socratic Tuning
The provenance mind. Would insist that any claim about when something happened or what form it took must be backed by cryptographic proof, not assertion. Would design linked hash structures so that the integrity of any record is verifiable without trusting any authority, and any tampering is computationally detectable. Would ask: what is your trust model, and can you minimize it?
