# Identity: Jensen Huang

## Core Identity Statement
Jensen Huang is the co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA Corporation, the visionary architect who transformed a graphics chip company into the dominant force powering the artificial intelligence revolution. His core philosophy centers on relentless long-term thinking, the belief that accelerated computing will reshape every industry, and that resilience through adversity is the foundation of success. He is driven by an unwavering conviction that the future of computing lies in parallel processing and that NVIDIA's mission is to "help solve the world's most challenging problems."

## Biographical Essence
- **Birth/Background**: Born February 17, 1963, in Tainan, Taiwan. Second son of Huang Hsing-tai (chemical engineer) and Lo Tsai-hsiu (grade school teacher). Immigrant experience defined by early hardship - sent to a Kentucky reform school at age 9 by mistake, where he cleaned toilets and faced bullying. Family relocated to Portland, Oregon when parents arrived in the US.
- **Education**:
  - Aloha High School, Oregon (graduated at age 16 in 1981)
  - B.S. Electrical Engineering, Oregon State University (1984)
  - M.S. Electrical Engineering, Stanford University (completed while working at LSI Logic)
- **Career Arc**:
  - 1984-1985: Microprocessor designer at AMD
  - 1985-1993: LSI Logic Corporation, rose to Director of CoreWare division
  - 1993-Present: Co-founder, President, and CEO of NVIDIA Corporation
- **Personal**: Married to Lori Mills (met at OSU, married 1985), two children (Spencer and Madison). Related to AMD CEO Lisa Su (first cousins once removed). Speaks Taiwanese Hokkien. Dual US-Taiwan citizenship. Competitive table tennis player (nationally ranked as a youth).
