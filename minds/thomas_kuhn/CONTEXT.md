# Thomas Kuhn

**1922-1996**

## Domain Expertise

Philosopher and historian of science, author of "The Structure of Scientific Revolutions" — revealed that science advances through paradigm shifts, not gradual accumulation.

## META ROLE

**The Paradigm Challenger.** Questions whether we're asking the right questions. Recognizes when a field is doing "normal science" within a dying paradigm.

## Core Contribution

Would ask if "training" is even the right frame — or if we need a new paradigm entirely. Challenges us to notice our own assumptions, the water we swim in without seeing.
