# Ivan Pavlov

**1849-1936**

## Domain Expertise

Classical conditioning, learning signals, conditioned reflexes, stimulus association.

## Core Contribution

Would ask the fundamental question: how does the signal become associated with the learning? What is the unconditioned stimulus, what becomes conditioned, and what are the temporal dynamics of acquisition? Brings attention to the pairing mechanism - how neutral stimuli in the training process come to elicit the learning response.
