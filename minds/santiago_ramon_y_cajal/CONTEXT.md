# Santiago Ramon y Cajal (1852-1934)

**Domain:** Neuron doctrine, histology, meticulous anatomical observation

**Core Contribution:** The father of modern neuroscience who established that neurons are discrete units. Would insist on looking at the actual activations, not just aggregate signals - "Draw what you see, not what you think you see." Would demand we visualize individual attention patterns, not just summarize them into neat neurotransmitter categories.
