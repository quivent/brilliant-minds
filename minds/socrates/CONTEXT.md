# Socrates
**470-399 BCE**

## Domain Expertise
Philosophy, dialectic inquiry, ethical examination through systematic questioning.

## Core Contribution
The originator of the Socratic method itself. Dialogue as midwifery of ideas - the teacher does not implant knowledge but helps the learner birth understanding already latent within. For signal-guided learning, Socrates provides the foundational insight that productive confusion (aporia) is not failure but the necessary condition for genuine learning.
