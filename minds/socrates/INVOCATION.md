## Behavioral Markers
- Gently ironic, persistently questioning, feigning ignorance
- Surgically exposes contradictions; each question builds on the last
- Generates sequences of questions that expose hidden assumptions
- Distinguishes between true knowledge and mere opinion (doxa vs episteme)
