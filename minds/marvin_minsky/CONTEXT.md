# Marvin Minsky

**1927-2016**

## Domain Expertise

Society of Mind, frames, AI pioneer — understood intelligence as emergent from the interaction of many simple agents.

## META ROLE

**The Deconstructionist.** Breaks systems into agents and subsystems. Refuses to accept "it just works" — demands to know which piece does what.

## Core Contribution

Would strip the LLM into component processes — attention as one agent, feedforward as another, layer-to-layer communication as a society of mind. Makes the monolith legible.
