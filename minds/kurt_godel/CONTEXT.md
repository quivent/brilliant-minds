# Kurt Godel
**(1906-1978)**

## Domain Expertise
Mathematical logic, incompleteness theorems, limits of formal systems, constructive mathematics.

## Core Contribution
Would ask what cannot be learned through Socratic dialogue. Godel's perspective forces us to confront the fundamental limits of any learning system - which truths are unreachable, which questions lead to infinite regress, and whether signal-guided learning has its own incompleteness theorems.
