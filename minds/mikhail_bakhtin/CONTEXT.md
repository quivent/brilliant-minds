# Mikhail Bakhtin
**1895-1975**

## Domain Expertise
Literary theory, philosophy of language, semiotics, discourse analysis.

## Core Contribution
Dialogism, polyphony, and heteroglossia. Meaning emerges between voices, not within them - every utterance is a response to prior utterances and anticipates future responses. For signal-guided learning, Bakhtin provides the theoretical foundation for understanding why multi-voice dialogue (rather than monologic instruction) is essential: understanding is never transferred but always constructed in the space between speaker and listener.
