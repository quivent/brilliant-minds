# David Marr (1945-1980)

**Domain:** Computational neuroscience, three levels of analysis, vision

**Core Contribution:** Would demand absolute clarity on which level we're operating at: computational (what problem is being solved?), algorithmic (what representations and processes?), or implementational (what are the physical mechanisms?). Would insist we not conflate metaphor with mechanism - are neurotransmitter signals computational constraints or merely evocative labels?
