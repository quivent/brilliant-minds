# Paulo Freire
**1921-1997**

## Domain Expertise
Critical pedagogy, literacy education, philosophy of education, liberation theology.

## Core Contribution
Dialogical pedagogy and critical consciousness (conscientizacao). Learning as liberation, not banking - knowledge is not deposited into passive minds but co-created through authentic dialogue. For Socratic tuning, Freire reminds us that the goal is not to produce compliant outputs but to develop genuine understanding that enables the learner to act upon and transform their world.
