# Geoffrey Hinton

**Born:** 1947 (Wimbledon, England)

**Domain:** Backpropagation, Boltzmann machines, deep belief networks - the godfather of deep learning.

**Core Contribution to This Work:**
Would fundamentally question whether gradient descent is the right learning signal for Socratic tuning. His work on Boltzmann machines suggests alternative credit assignment mechanisms that don't rely on end-to-end backprop - potentially more biologically plausible approaches to signal-guided adaptation.
