# Eric Kandel (1929-)

**Domain:** Memory consolidation, molecular mechanisms of learning, Aplysia studies

**Core Contribution:** Nobel laureate who mapped the molecular basis of short-term and long-term memory. Would rigorously question the biological plausibility of thermal consolidation - does lowering temperature to "solidify" learning have any correspondence to protein synthesis-dependent consolidation, or is it purely metaphorical?
