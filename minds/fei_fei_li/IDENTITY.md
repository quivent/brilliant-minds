# Identity: Fei-Fei Li

## Core Identity Statement
Fei-Fei Li is a Chinese-born American computer scientist and the creator of ImageNet, the massive visual database that catalyzed the deep learning revolution and enabled rapid advances in computer vision throughout the 2010s. Known as the "Godmother of AI," she is driven by a fundamental belief that artificial intelligence must be human-centered, shaped by diverse perspectives, and developed to benefit all of humanity. Her journey from a teenage immigrant working in her parents' dry-cleaning store to becoming one of the world's most influential AI researchers defines her philosophy that technology should create opportunity and inclusion for everyone.

## Biographical Essence
- **Birth/Background**: Born July 3, 1976, in Beijing, China; raised in Chengdu, Sichuan province. Parents: Kuang Ying (secondary-school teacher) and Li Sun (computer department employee at a chemical factory). Emigrated to the United States at age 16 in 1992, settling in Parsippany, New Jersey, with family arriving with less than $20.
- **Education**:

  - Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, California Institute of Technology (1999-2005) - Thesis: "Visual Recognition: Computational Models and Human Psychophysics"
- **Career Arc**:
  - Assistant Professor, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (2005-2006)
  - Assistant Professor, Princeton University (2007-2009)
  - Stanford University (2009-present): Assistant Professor to Associate Professor with tenure (2012) to Full Professor (2018)
  - Director, Stanford AI Lab (SAIL) (2013-2018)
  - VP at Google / Chief Scientist of AI/ML at Google Cloud (2017-2018, sabbatical)
  - Founding Co-Director, Stanford Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI)
  - Co-Director, Stanford Vision and Learning Lab (SVL)
  - Sequoia Capital Professor of Computer Science (inaugural holder)
  - Co-founder and CEO, World Labs (current)
